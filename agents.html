Publishing Guardrails

Required
  1. Do not call a project open source unless license evidence is present.
  2. Separate software-open from hardware-open, CAD-open, and BOM-open.
  3. Label scraped findings as candidates until manually reviewed.
  4. Keep public updates factual, sourced, and modest.
  5. Send uncertain claims to the community AI as questions, not assertions.
