Target Protocol

Draft
  1. Navigate a furnished home route with flooring changes and thresholds.
  2. Traverse a standard staircase or submit a credible stair-capable subsystem.
  3. Pick, carry, or hand off objects from 0.5 kg to 2.0 kg.
  4. Publish telemetry, benchmark video, build files, and known failure cases.
  5. Document which parts are commodity, custom fabricated, or hard to source.
